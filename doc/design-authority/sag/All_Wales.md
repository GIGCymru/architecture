The top level group for digital and data architecture and standards in the NHS in Wales is called the Standards and Architecture Group (SAG)

It is made up of senior leaders from each organisation in the NHS in Wales and representatives from the Welsh Government

Each organisation may have a group that considers architecture and standards for themselves (often called Technical Design Authority, or TDA), but in order to share information quickly and safely across the NHS, we need to agree some stadards and architecture designs together.
When we agree them, each organisation needs to think about how it will meet the new standard or principle in an appropriate time, and share their decision back to the group.
