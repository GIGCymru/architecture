# TDA Terms of Reference

## Introduction

The Public Health Wales (PHW) Technical Design Authority (TDA) is an expert
advisory group that provides collaborative assurance to the Data & Digital
Design Authority (DDDA) that PHW's architecture, patterns and data & digital
standards are robust, complete and in line with national and statutory
standards.

The TDA meets **monthly**. Extraordinary meetings may be called by the Chair.

## Objectives

* Provide expert members for the digital speciality sub-groups across Wales
  (e.g. the Welsh Information Standards Group and the Welsh Interoperability
  Group) and a representative member for the All-Wales Standards and
  Architecture Group (SAG).
* Represent the needs of PHW when national groups determine patterns and
  standards that apply across NHS Wales.
* Inform PHW, through the DDDA, of additions to the national target
  architecture, architectural patterns and digital, data and technology
  standards that will apply locally.
* Share information transparently about which parts of PHW will meet new
  standards and patterns, and the timetable for their natural adoption.
* Ensure PHW's technical standards, architecture, designs and processes align
  with national standards and the national target architecture, as defined by
  [SAG](../nhs-wales/sag-terms-of-reference.md), its speciality sub-groups
  (such as the Welsh Information Standards Board) and Welsh Health Circulars
  issued by Welsh Government.
* Work with other PHW groups and artefacts to support adherence to adopted
  standards, including the Digital Review & Advisory Group, the User-Centred
  Delivery Flow, Ignite, the Service Management Framework and the Project and
  Programme management group.

## Responsibilities

* Contribute to a permanent register of the digital, data and technology
  standards and patterns that apply in NHS Wales.
* Provide expert guidance on digital, data and technical design across PHW.
* Ensure the clinical safety aspect of recommended standards.
* Through the sub-groups, consider new standards on behalf of PHW and make
  recommendations to SAG on which to adopt.
* Confirm which nationally adopted standards must be met, and provide
  guidance and assistance on assessing systems against them.
* Create and share with SAG the lists of systems and services that must meet
  each new standard, including the time until each applies (usually at
  upgrade or replacement).
* When asked by the DDDA in exceptional circumstances, advise on new and
  emerging technologies, including their potential impact on PHW and whether
  they should be adopted.

## Decisions

* Decisions are made by consensus between members in attendance and any
  nominated delegates.
* Members come prepared and empowered to express views and make
  recommendations on behalf of the profession they represent.
* A recommendation for a new standard should be agreed by all. Failing that,
  a simple majority applies. The Chair does not vote, except to cast the
  deciding vote in the event of a tie.

## Membership

Members are senior technical experts with a broad range of knowledge and
experience, so that the TDA can provide guidance and oversight on all aspects
of technical design. Additional members may be co-opted as new needs are
identified.

* Chair
* Vice Chair
* Cloud Architect
* Cyber Security
* Databases
* Data Engineering
* Data Science and Analysis
* Data Security
* Digital Clinical Safety
* IT Operations
* Project Management Office (PMO)
* Systems Architect
* Systems Development
* User-Centred Design

The Chair advises on current digital and data practices and standards,
attends SAG on behalf of PHW, recommends new standards to the DDDA and ensures
the TDA stays aligned with the Data and Digital Strategy.

## Meetings

* **Quorum:** the Chair or a delegated Vice Chair, and at least 50% of members
  or their empowered delegates.
* **Delegates:** members may send a delegate, who is assumed to have the
  member's expertise. The secretariat should be notified in advance.
* **Invitations:** other users, senior leaders or subject matter experts may
  be invited depending on the subject matter.
* **Standing agenda:**
    * Welcome and apologies
    * Review minutes and actions from the last meeting
    * Updates from SAG on new national standards or architectural requirements
    * Reports from speciality sub-groups developing standards
    * Requests for new standards
    * Risks and issues
    * Recommendations and escalations to the DDDA
    * Any other business
* **Agenda and papers:** distributed at least one week before each meeting.
  Papers must not contain restricted information, so they can be shared openly
  within PHW and with associated Boards.
* **Secretariat:** minutes are available within a week of each meeting,
  setting out decisions and actions. Meetings are recorded only to produce
  accurate minutes, and recordings are not stored.

## Scope

**In Scope:**

* Digital, data and technology standards, patterns and architecture across PHW
* Alignment with the national target architecture and national standards
* Clinical safety aspects of recommended data, digital and technical standards
* New and emerging technologies, when requested by the DDDA

**Out of Scope:**

* Project and programme management standards (managed by the Portfolio
  Management Office, which has a standing invitation to the TDA)
* Clinical standards (managed by the relevant clinical specialties). New data
  standards that cross over with clinical areas are routed through the Welsh
  Information Standards Board, which includes clinical oversight.

## Reporting, Review and Closure

* The TDA reports to the DDDA and links to SAG and all speciality sub-groups.
  It seeks assurance from the DDDA by reporting on its work and
  recommendations.
* Membership and these terms of reference are reviewed annually.
* The TDA may close following a significant change to or replacement of the
  DDDA, or a change in PHW's governance and assurance requirements.
